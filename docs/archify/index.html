Stage 01 of 14

Diagrams of the registrar's office that fits in one repository

Ten views of the system as it exists today: a modular monolith with four domains, one relational database and nothing else. Each opens on its own page, with light and dark themes, search, per-element focus and guided walkthroughs.

No diagram describes what the product will be. They all describe what it is — including the decisions that hurt on purpose, so that the thirteen stages that follow happen because of evidence and not because of the calendar.

Architecture and boundaries

The layout of the whole system and the rule that keeps it from rotting: no module sees the inside of another, and the build tooling fails anyone who tries.

  1. 01

    The registrar's office that fits in one repository

    Architecture

    Browser, web layer, the four domains and one PostgreSQL. Shows the permanent rent and the list of what was deliberately left out.

  2. 02

    The boundary between modules

    Architecture

    The permitted dependency graph and the three rules checked by bun run check. It is the decision that makes Stage 14 an extraction rather than a rewrite.

  3. 10

    Where the data lives — and what comes out of it

    Data flow

    From external input to the source of truth, and from there to report card, board, log and dump. Shows what the log never carries and why a backup only counts once restored.

Operations from the inside

What happens between the click and the commit, the lifecycle of the system's central record, and the synchronous operation that plants the Stage 05 pain.

  1. 03

    A whole write, end to end

    Sequence

    The teacher posts the class group's grades. Correlation, session, idempotency, boundary crossing and a single commit point, on one screen.

  2. 04

    Enrollment lifecycle

    States

    Active, transferred, cancelled and completed. Why transferring is a single operation, and why the partial unique index is what guarantees the rule.

  3. 05

    Term closing

    Process

    The check that blocks the browser on purpose. Closing one class group takes seconds; closing the network's forty takes minutes — and that number is what justifies the queue.

Journeys by role

One journey for each of the system's four roles, from sign-in to the effect in the database, with what each role may do and what is refused to them.

  1. 06

    Network administrator

    Journey

    Creates schools, defines the academic year and invites users. The temporary password appears once on screen, because e-mail delivery only arrives at Stage 04.

  2. 07

    Registrar

    Journey

    Registers, links a guardian, enrols and transfers. A second active enrollment in the same year is refused by the database, not by the screen.

  3. 08

    Teacher

    Journey

    Posts grades in batch, records roll call by day and closes the term. Only sees the class groups where they are the assigned teacher; any other identifier answers 404.

  4. 09

    Guardian

    Journey

    Board, on-screen report card and day-by-day attendance. Marking as read is a form, never a side effect of opening the page — without that, the 12 % rate would be a lie.

Each diagram sits next to the specification that produced it, under the same name with the .json extension. To re-edit one, change the specification and regenerate the HTML with archify — the delivered file's content is verified before it is written.

The files are self-contained: they open over file://, with no server and no network request at all. Horizontal containment was checked at 1440, 1600, 1920 and 2048 pixels; the diagrams with lanes take more than one screen vertically and need scrolling to read the conclusion cards.
